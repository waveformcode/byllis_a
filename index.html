01 The tables

Break, Play, Repeat.

Billy’s has always been a billiards pub first. The tables are the heart of the place. Rack up a casual game with friends, or challenge the regulars.

02 After the break

Pints, pads & the match.

  1. 02.1

    At the bar

    Bitter IPAs, golden lagers and dark stouts on offer, plus all your usual drinks. Hungry mid-game? Toasties and pizza to keep you going.

  2. 02.2

    Game on

    Two PlayStation 5s, loaded with FIFA, Tekken and plenty more. No booking, just grab a controller when one’s free. There’s also a dartboard and a classic pinball machine for between rounds.

  3. 02.3

    Live on the screen

    Super League and Champions League on every match day, with the sound up for the big games. For basketball fans, EuroLeague and the Greek league. Also live tennis and fight nights.
